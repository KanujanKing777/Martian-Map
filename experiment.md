# Mars Mission Experimental Protocol & Target Coordinates

This mission plan outlines high-priority scientific and engineering experiments derived from orbital, lander, and rover datasets (including NASA's MRO, Curiosity, Perseverance, InSight, and MAVEN). Each experiment is paired with precise Martian planetocentric coordinates and target site rationale.

| Experiments & Protocols | Precise Location (Latitude & Longitude) | Target Region & Scientific Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subsurface Ice Mining & Purification**<br>• Drill 1–2 meters through surface regolith to access buried ice sheets.<br>• Test thermal extraction rigs and filtration systems to remove toxic perchlorates for potable water and fuel. | **Latitude: 39.800° N**<br>**Longitude: 202.100° E**<br>(Candidate Site AP-1)<br><br>Alt. Site (AP-9):<br>**Latitude: 40.024° N**<br>**Longitude: 203.331° E** | **Arcadia Planitia (Northern Lowlands)**<br>Radar data confirms widespread, shallow water-ice sheets under flat, low-elevation terrain (-3.9 km), ideal for safe heavy landings and ISRU. |
| **Scaled-Up Rocket Fuel & Oxygen Production**<br>• Operate a high-capacity Sabatier reactor combining extracted water and atmospheric CO2.<br>• Produce and liquefy methane (CH4) and oxygen (LOX) propellant for Earth return. | **Latitude: 39.800° N**<br>**Longitude: 202.100° E**<br>(Candidate Site AP-1) | **Arcadia Planitia (Northern Lowlands)**<br>Co-locating fuel synthesis directly above shallow ice deposits minimizes transport overhead and validates closed-loop human survival systems. |
| **Regolith Sintering & Radiation Shielding**<br>• Compact local soil into modular bags or microwave-sinter regolith into structural bricks.<br>• Measure attenuation of galactic cosmic rays and solar particle events. | **Latitude: 39.890° N**<br>**Longitude: 192.030° E**<br>(Candidate Site EM-16) | **Arcadia Planitia / Erebus Montes Border**<br>Low elevation provides a thicker atmospheric column for natural radiation reduction while offering abundant fine-grained basaltic regolith. |
| **Deep Core Biosignature Drilling**<br>• Extract intact rock cores 2–5 meters below the surface using a rotary drill.<br>• Analyze shielded samples via gas chromatography-mass spectrometry for complex amino acids, lipids, or microfossils. | **Latitude: 18.4663° N**<br>**Longitude: 77.4298° E**<br>(Perseverance Landing Zone)<br><br>Delta Front Target:<br>**Latitude: 18.447° N**<br>**Longitude: 77.452° E** | **Jezero Crater (Western Isidis Planitia)**<br>Confirmed ancient lakebed and river delta containing smectite clays, carbonates, and simple organic molecules preserved from 3.5 billion years ago. |
| **Perchlorate Soil Remediation & Agriculture**<br>• Wash delta clays and introduce perchlorate-reducing bacteria inside a sealed greenhouse.<br>• Test crop viability (potatoes, soybeans, spirulina) in detoxified Martian soil. | **Latitude: 18.4663° N**<br>**Longitude: 77.4298° E**<br>(Jezero Crater Floor) | **Jezero Crater (Western Isidis Planitia)**<br>Nutrient-rich clay minerals mixed with trace elements provide an ideal substrate to test biological soil detoxification and closed-loop farming. |
| **Lava Tube Habitat Mapping & Shielding**<br>• Deploy autonomous drones and ground-penetrating radar into collapsed skylight pits.<br>• Measure internal thermal stability, structural integrity, and natural radiation shielding. | **Latitude: 3.433° S (-3.433°)**<br>**Longitude: 236.314° E**<br>(Arsia Mons Skylight Pit)<br><br>Secondary Pit:<br>**Latitude: 3.069° S (-3.069°)**<br>**Longitude: 236.062° E** | **Arsia Mons Flank (Tharsis Montes)**<br>Orbital HiRISE imagery confirms deep vertical skylight entrances leading into massive underground volcanic caverns capable of housing permanent bases. |
| **Multi-Station Seismic Network**<br>• Deploy a triangular array of broadband seismometers spaced several kilometers apart.<br>• Triangulate marsquake epicenters and map subsurface magma chambers or liquid aquifers. | **Latitude: 8.350° S (-8.350°)**<br>**Longitude: 239.910° E** (120.09° W)<br>(Arsia Mons Regional Array) | **Arsia Mons / Tharsis Volcanic Province**<br>Building on NASA InSight data, a multi-node seismic array around Mars's youngest major shield volcano resolves deep crustal and mantle structure. |
| **Electrostatic Dust Mitigation & Ballooning**<br>• Test electrodynamic dust shields on spacesuits, visors, and solar arrays.<br>• Launch instrumented weather balloons to profile boundary-layer wind shear and dust lifting. | **Latitude: 39.800° N, Lon: 202.100° E** (Arcadia Planitia)<br>&<br>**Latitude: 18.466° N, Lon: 77.430° E** (Jezero Crater) | **Multi-Site Atmospheric Profiling**<br>Comparing mid-latitude northern plains with equatorial basin meteorology provides critical entry, descent, and landing (EDL) wind data. |

*Coordinate System Note: All coordinates follow the standard IAU/USGS Planetocentric system (+East longitude, 0° to 360°), referenced from NASA Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (HiRISE/SHARAD) landing site assessments and Mars 2020 telemetry.*
