Mars Atlas is an interactive Mars surface explorer and route-planning app centered on Jezero Crater. Users can search named features or coordinates, inspect famous locations, compare Optical, Topographic, Geophysical (USGS geology), and THEMIS day/night Thermal layers, and share a map view. It also provides a Street View-like ground-level exploration view of Jezero Crater. For mission planning, the Missions panel presents eight experiments and their target sites; selecting a target starts route planning from the current map center. The Python backend uses A* to find a least-cost path using MOLA/HRSC elevation-derived slope and roughness, with TES thermal inertia contributing when available. Slopes above 20° are blocked, while hazards without suitable numeric datasets are omitted, so routes are planning estimates rather than safety guarantees. By bringing orbital context, surface-level exploration, mission targets, and terrain-aware routing into one app, Mars Atlas helps users explore Mars and plan potential traverses.
